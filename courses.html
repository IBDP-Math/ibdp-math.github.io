Courses

All four DP Mathematics courses cover the same five topics, to different depths and with different emphasis.

AA SL

For students who enjoy algebraic manipulation and want a strong base for maths-heavy university courses.

AA SL worksheets
  • Number and algebra
  • Functions
  • Geometry and trigonometry
  • Statistics and probability
  • Calculus

AA HL

For students heading towards mathematics, physics or engineering. Includes proof and more advanced calculus.

AA HL worksheets
  • Number and algebra
  • Functions
  • Geometry and trigonometry
  • Statistics and probability
  • Calculus

AI SL

For students who want maths as a practical tool: modelling, statistics and technology-supported problem solving.

AI SL worksheets
  • Number and algebra
  • Functions
  • Geometry and trigonometry
  • Statistics and probability
  • Calculus

AI HL

For students heading towards economics, social or life sciences who need deeper modelling and statistics.

AI HL worksheets
  • Number and algebra
  • Functions
  • Geometry and trigonometry
  • Statistics and probability
  • Calculus
