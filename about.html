About

Who writes these resources and how they are made.

I am Avinash Billa, an IB Diploma Programme Mathematics teacher based in Hyderabad, India. I write these worksheets for my own DP classes and share them here so any IB student or teacher can use them.

Every worksheet is original. Questions are written in the style of IB assessment, from short skill checks to longer multi-part problems, and each comes with complete worked solutions.

New worksheets are added regularly, starting with Applications and Interpretation SL.
