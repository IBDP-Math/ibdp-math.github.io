Five topics across every course

The DP syllabus is built on the same five areas. Each worksheet is tagged with one, so you can revise topic by topic.

Number and algebra

Sequences, exponents, logarithms, financial maths.

\(S_n=\frac{n}{2}(u_1+u_n)\)

Functions

Graphs, transformations and modelling with functions.

\(f(x)=a(x-h)^2+k\)

Geometry and trigonometry

Triangles, 3D shapes, circles and trig functions.

\(\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}\)

Statistics and probability

Data, distributions, correlation and testing.

\(\bar{x}=\frac{\sum x}{n}\)

Calculus

Rates of change, optimisation, area under curves.

\(\frac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}\)

How to use a worksheet

Attempt it on paper

Questions move from routine skills to multi-part, exam-style problems. Work through them without looking ahead.

Check the worked solutions

Fix the gaps

Mark where your working went off track, then redo those questions a few days later.
